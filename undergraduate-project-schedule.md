# Undergraduate project schedule

## Overview

Each year the lab takes on undergraduate project students. Their main lab day is **Tuesday**, which is the primary training day for whichever technique is scheduled that week. Lab members are assigned specific weeks to lead training; the timetable for the current cohort is below.

---

## Responsibilities for the week you are leading

**Supervision.** You are the undergraduate's point of contact for the whole week, not just on Tuesday. They cannot be in the lab unsupported at any point. You are responsible for either being present with them or arranging another lab member to cover. Budget approximately 15 hours across the week.

**Training.** Where possible, have them work on their actual samples rather than practice runs. This keeps the work meaningful and moves the project forward.

**AFHEA log.** If you are working towards your Associate Fellowship of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA), use your week as a teaching opportunity and keep a log of the techniques and approaches you used. The university will fund your AFHEA application. More information: [Associate Fellowship, Advance HE](https://advance-he.ac.uk/fellowship/associate-fellowship/).

If you cannot cover your assigned week, or would like to take on an additional week, contact Eamonn or Hollie to rearrange.
