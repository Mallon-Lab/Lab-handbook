- Arrange your access with a staff member the day before experiments. Don't just show up, or arrange a time with little notice.
- Working hours are between 9am and 5pm.

## Wet lab rules

### Health and safety

- You will have a general lab induction before starting your project. This must be completed before any work in lab 519.
- All COSHH forms for reagents and protocols you use are in the [risk assessments](risk-assessments.md). Familiarise yourself with the risks and mitigations before starting any experiment.
- Wear a lab coat and gloves at all times during lab work. If you don't have a lab coat, let Christian know. Gloves are at the edge of the bench on the far side of the lab. If your size isn't available or you have material allergies, inform a staff member.
- The department enforces a **one glove rule** in corridors. Remove one glove when leaving lab 519 and open doors with your ungloved hand.
- If you think a reagent is running low, email Christian to order replacements. Allow about a week for delivery — plan ahead.

### Cleanliness

- Clean the bench surface with Chemgene before and after experiments.
- The black bins by the sink are for paper towels only.
- Dispose of lab waste and insect waste in the autoclave bins only.
- Uncontaminated recyclable waste (plastic film around pipette tips, cardboard boxes) can go in the recycling bins in the corridor or in the bins up the ramp between the Hodgkin building and Attenborough Arts Centre.
- Clear lab benches after experiments.
